Buyer Profiles API

Read the buyer profiles (My Buyers) of the brokerages your credential covers: a paginated list and the full detail of each buyer, as JSON over HTTPS.

  • Read-only · GET
  • 2 endpoints
  • 100 buyers per page max
  • Updated October 21, 2026

Requests authenticate as described in Authentication. Your credential must include the /buyers/profiles routes.

Endpoints

GET/buyers/profiles

List buyer profiles

Returns a page of buyers, newest first by default. Every parameter is optional.

ParameterValuesDefaultEffect
statusactive · inactive · allactiveActive = active or non-verified, target purchase date not yet passed. Inactive = inactive, or target date passed.
brokerage_idintegerall yoursOne of your brokerages; any other returns 403.
brand_idintegerall yoursYour brokerages in that brand; another brand returns 403.
agent_idinteger—Buyers assigned to that agent.
office_idinteger—Buyers created under that office. Not combinable with agent_id.
ziptext, ≤ 10—Buyers with that zip code among their search areas.
property_type_idinteger—Buyers looking for that property type.
qtext, ≤ 255—Search in the buyer's first name, last name or email.
sortbuyer_id · first_name · last_name · email · created · updated · target_price · maximum_price · target_purchase_date · star_ratingbuyer_idSort field.
dirasc · descdesc for buyer_id, else ascSort direction.
pageinteger ≥ 11Page number.
per_page1–10025Page size.
GET/buyers/profiles/{buyer_id}

Get one buyer profile

Returns the full profile of one buyer. A buyer outside your brokerages returns 404, the same as one that does not exist.

Response format

The list returns {"data": [...], "meta": {...}}; the detail returns {"data": {...}}. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD, timestamps are ISO 8601 in UTC, and a missing value is null.

Pagination. meta carries page, per_page, total (buyers matching your filters) and last_page. Keep requesting pages until page equals last_page.

Fields in the list and the detail

FieldMeaning
buyer_idBuyer profile id.
status, is_activeactive, inactive, non_verified, deleted or unlisted. is_active is true for active and non-verified.
userThe buyer: id, first and last name, email, primary phone.
agent, brand, brokerage, officeWho the buyer belongs to, each with id and name.
bedrooms, is_studio, bathroomsBedroom and bathroom criteria.
target_price, maximum_priceBudget, in US dollars.
target_purchase_dateWhen the buyer plans to buy.
prequalifiednone, pre_qualified, pre_approved or cash.
star_ratingThe agent's rating of the buyer.
matching_email_alertListing alerts: off, to_agent or to_buyer.
source_type, sourceOrigin: MANUAL, IMPORT, INQUIRY, SAVED_SEARCH, SAVED_PROPERTY, SHOWING, OPEN_HOUSE, FEED or UNKNOWN, plus the feed or import name.
createdWhen the profile was created.

Extra fields in the detail

  • Buyer secondary phone, address, city, state, zip
  • Assignment team_id, office_id
  • Property square footage, property age, parking, floors, lot size, full and half bathrooms
  • Financing down payment, monthly payment, prequalified by and amount, payment methods, buyer types, must sell first
  • Notes comment, private_comments
  • Agreement buyer_rep_agreement: signed, expire date, file name
  • Search areas locations (zip, city, county, state), property_type_ids, school_districts, townships
  • Audit created by, updated by, updated, first and last ingestion time

Errors

The common statuses, quota and timeout are in Errors and limits. On these routes:

StatusWhen
400A parameter is invalid: wrong type, out of range, per_page over 100, office_id with agent_id, or a brokerage_id outside the brand_id sent.
403brokerage_id or brand_id outside your scope, or a credential without the /buyers/profiles routes.
404The buyer does not exist or is outside your scope.

Known limitation. On very large scopes (over a million buyers), the q search and sorting by first_name, last_name or email can take longer than 29 seconds and return 504. We are improving it. Until then, narrow the request with agent_id, office_id, brokerage_id or zip, and keep the default sort.
